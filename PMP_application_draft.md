- Project financial scope: High-level financial scope based on approximately USD 3,500 revenue per successful intervention and approximately USD 1,000 average cost per successful intervention

- Industry: Education technology / telecommunications infrastructure / field operations
- Reference / verifier:
  - Fernando Lopez Caminal, CTO, fernando.lopez@ocp.tech
  - Fernando Huttin, PMO Officer, fernando.huttin@ocp.tech

#### Project Objective

Install and maintain Wi-Fi infrastructure across approximately 9,700 schools in Argentina for Educ.Ar and the Ministry of Education, reaching a target rate of 700 successful field interventions per month while maintaining operational control, delivery quality, contractor coordination and financial traceability.

#### Role and Responsibilities

As Senior Project Manager, I was accountable for project planning, execution coordination, stakeholder management, operational governance, risk and issue management, field execution follow-up, contractor coordination, progress reporting, delivery control and customer-facing operational alignment.

I led an interdisciplinary team composed of one controller, three infrastructure coordinators responsible for coordinating logistics, materials and equipment deliveries with approximately 50 contractor accounts, five support desk technicians responsible for field intervention support and follow-up, and two operations coordinators responsible for planning and customer operational interface.

I coordinated the hybrid delivery model. Procurement, resource provisioning, delivery logistics, closure activities and billing follow-up were managed through a waterfall approach. Field intervention planning and execution were managed through agile cycles, with each sprint delivering batches of completed and successful school interventions.

#### Outcome

Each school intervention was treated as a formal deliverable, including installed and operational equipment. Additional deliverables included contractor agreements and customer billing certifications. A successful intervention was defined according to an extensive acceptance manual approved by both parties.

The project completed approximately 7,500 successful school interventions. The remaining planned interventions were cancelled by Educ.Ar due to closed schools or errors in the official school records. The project reached the target of 700 successful field interventions per month and achieved peak performance of up to 1,100 successful interventions in a month. Performance was tracked through successful monthly interventions, target achievement and high-level financial indicators per intervention.

#### Notes

This is a strong PMP experience entry because it includes a defined customer, national-scale scope, measurable delivery targets, budget/unit economics, a direct leadership role, a multidisciplinary team and a hybrid delivery methodology.
